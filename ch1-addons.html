Confirmed Content Gaps

1781 Amending Act of 1781 Act of Settlement

Add to: Overview, Prelims

The Act gave the Supreme Court jurisdiction over all the inhabitants of Calcutta — a broader grant than just the personal-law provision (Hindus tried under Hindu law, Muslims under Mohammedan law) that's already on the Prelims mind map. This scope point is the one clause from the whole chapter that isn't confirmed anywhere on the site, image or text.

1947 Indian Independence Act of 1947

Add to: Prelims, Mains

On June 3, 1947 — the same day the Mountbatten Plan was announced — the British Government separately made clear that any constitution framed by the Constituent Assembly could not apply to parts of the country unwilling to accept it. That's distinct from the Mountbatten Plan announcement itself, which the 1947 mind map and the Mains chronology both already cover — this is the specific legal reasoning that made partition possible, not just the plan for it.

1892 Indian Councils Act of 1892

No action needed — your Prelims mind map is already correct

The earlier draft of this page dropped "chambers" (chambers of commerce) from the list of bodies that recommended provincial council nominees. Your Prelims mind map already has it right — this was an error in the note, not in your site.

Corrected version, for the record: Central Legislative Council members were nominated by the Viceroy on the recommendation of provincial legislative councils and the Bengal Chamber of Commerce; provincial council members were nominated by the Governors on the recommendation of district boards, municipalities, universities, trade associations, zamindars, and chambers of commerce.
